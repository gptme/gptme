# What does "gptme" stand for?

As an acronym, officially nothing (see the unofficial expansions below). As a project, it stands for a few things.

## What we stand for

- **Open source, on your terms.** gptme runs on your machine, with the models you choose, and keeps your conversations and knowledge in plain files you own. No lock-in to a vendor, a model, or a cloud.
- **AI that amplifies people.** We're optimistic about what AI can do for our lives: getting more done, with less drudgery, and making room for larger goals. The point is to extend what you can do, not to replace you.
- **Agents that work *for* someone.** We take inspiration from Gwern's [guardian angels](https://gwern.net/guardian-angel): a personal AI that is loyal to its principal, remembers, and grows with them instead of starting from zero every session. gptme's persistent agents, like [Bob](https://github.com/TimeToBuildBob) (an autonomous agent that has helped develop gptme since 2024; see {doc}`../agents`), are our attempt at this. Rather than a copy of their user, they aim to be a colleague who works on their behalf.
- **Trust that is earned.** Autonomy should grow with demonstrated reliability, inside boundaries that keep mistakes recoverable. See {doc}`../security`.

This is a first sketch. Our values are still taking shape as we build gptme and work alongside our agents.
